\section{Conclusion}

In this project, we present a multimodal Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) pipeline with a focus on visual elements
through a dual-representation framework, where text and visual features are separated.
We utilize state-of-the-art pretrained foundational models (DINOv2 and Talk2DINO) as the embedder and retriever,
and achieve results on par with or slightly better compared to results presented in benchmark papers.
Our preliminary testing shows that MLLM-based captioning reaches 97.8\% accuracy,
and the DINOv2-Talk2DINO pipeline retrieves fine-grained matches, 
supporting our hypothesis that separate retrieval for different modalities achieves enhanced retrieval performance 
and improves grounding.

However, our pipeline still faces limitations in preprocessing overhead, theoretical bounds of dense retrieval,
and current MLLM deficits in visual perception.
Despite these limitations, our pipeline offers a solid foundation for generation grounding with retrieved visual evidence 
in high-stakes domains where generation accuracy is crucial.

Future work will explore more efficient preprocessing, agentic retrieval, domain-specific fine-tuning of embedders and retrievers,
and extending the pipeline to text-to-vision image generation tasks.
