\section{Retrieval and Generation Integration Tests}
\label{appendix:integrationtests}

We obtain a second corpus of another 4 documents retrieved from Wikipedia\footnote{The Wikipedia pages are linked as follows: \\
\url{https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fuxing_(train)} \\
\url{https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hexie_(train)} \\
\url{https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MTR_CRRC_Changchun_EMU} \\
\url{https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MTR_SP1900_EMU}
}.
From information on these pages, we handcraft a small dataset of 11 queries for testing end-to-end processing and generation,
to verify that our pipeline works as expected.

The 11 questions are as follows:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item What colors have been used on the livery of Fuxing trains?
    \item What is the CR450 and what is special about it?
    \item On which railway lines does the Fuxing train CR400 operate?
    \item What colors are the Hexie trains in?
    \item How many seats does the engineer's compartment on the CRH3 have?
    \item What is the front of the CRH1 train like?
    \item What is the colour scheme of the interior of the MTR CRRC Changchun train (also known as TML C-Train)?
    \item Why is the MTR CRRC Changchun EMU (TML C-Train) compared to the SP1900, and what differences do these two trains have in terms of appearance and interior?
    \item Where were the MTR CRRC Changchun EMU (TML C-Train) trainsets built? When did the trainsets fully enter service?
    \item What quantitative differences do first-class carriages of the SP1900 train have as compared to other carriages?
    \item What major incidents have happened to SP1900 trains?
\end{enumerate}

We perform zero-shot, text-only retrieval, and multimodal retrieval tests using this dataset, and experiment with different values of $k$ for top-$k$ retrieval.
