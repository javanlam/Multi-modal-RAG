\section{Summary of Mathematical Notations}
\label{appendix:mathnotations}

The following is a summary of all mathematical notations used in the formal task definition.

\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{} c c X @{}}
    \toprule
    \textbf{Symbol} & \textbf{Type} & \textbf{Description} \\
    \midrule
    \endhead
    $\mathcal{D}$ & Set & Set of all documents, $\mathcal{D} = \{ d^{(i)} \}_{i=1}^n$ \\
    $d^{(i)}$ & Document & The $i$-th document in the collection \\
    $t^{(i)}$ & Text & Raw text content of document $i$ \\
    $\mathcal{I}^{(i)}$ & Set & Set of images in document $i$, $\mathcal{I}^{(i)} = \{ m_j^{(i)} \}_{j=1}^{p}$ \\
    $m_j^{(i)}$ & Image & The $j$-th image in document $i$ \\
    $p_j^{(i)}$ & String & Caption for the $j$-th image in document $i$ \\
    $t^{(i)}_{m_j^{(i)}}$ & Text & Surrounding text context of image $m_j^{(i)}$ in document $i$ \\
    $x$ & Prompt string & Instruction prompt given to the MLLM for caption generation \\
    $\mathcal{P}^{(i)}$ & Set of strings & Set of generated captions for all images in document $i$ \\
    $\text{LLM}(\cdot)$ & Function & LLM used for processing knowledge graph and communities \\
    $\text{MLLM}(\cdot)$ & Function & Multimodal LLM used for captioning and answer generation \\
    $t_{\text{combined}}^{(i)}$ & Text & Full text after inserting captions into the original text \\
    $L$ & Integer & Chunk size in characters \\
    $O$ & Integer & Overlap length in characters between consecutive chunks \\
    $c_j^{(i)}$ & Text string & The $j$-th text chunk of document $i$ \\
    $m$ & Integer & Total number of chunks produced from a document \\
    $\mathbf{v}_j^{(i)}$ & Vector ($\mathbb{R}^d$) & Dense embedding of chunk $c_j^{(i)}$ obtained from text encoder $\mathcal{N}_{\text{text}}$ \\
    $\mathcal{N}_{\text{text}}$ & Function & Neural text embedding model (MiniLM) \\
    $\mathcal{S}$ & Vector database & External vector database storing all text chunk embeddings \\
    $\mathcal{G}$ & Graph & Knowledge graph $(N, E)$ used in the graph storage option \\
    $N$ & Set & Nodes in the knowledge graph (extracted entities) \\
    $E$ & Set & Edges in the knowledge graph (relationships between entities) \\
    $C$ & Set of sets & Communities detected in the knowledge graph, $C = \{C_1, C_2, \dots\}$ \\
    $C_{\text{summaries}}$ & Set of strings & Natural language summaries for each community, generated by an LLM \\
    $\mathbf{g}_j^{(i)}$ & Vector ($\mathbb{R}^d$) & DINOv2 embedding of image $m_j^{(i)}$ \\
    $\mathcal{S}_{\text{images}}$ & Multi‑vector DB & Database storing tuples $(\mathbf{g}_j^{(i)}, p_j^{(i)})$ where $p_j^{(i)}$ is the image caption \\
    $\mathcal{Q}$ & Tuple & User query, $\mathcal{Q} = (q_{\text{text}}, \mathcal{Q}_{\text{images}})$ \\
    $q_{\text{text}}$ & String & Text part of the user query \\
    $\mathcal{Q}_{\text{images}}$ & Set of images & Images provided by the user as part of the query \\
    $k$ & Integer & Number of top items retrieved (e.g., top‑$k$ chunks or communities) \\
    $\mathrm{sim}(\cdot, \cdot)$ & Function & Similarity metric (cosine similarity) \\
    $\mathcal{C}^R$ & Set of vectors & Retrieved text chunks (their embeddings) from dense retrieval \\
    $\mathcal{I}^R$ & Set of vectors & Retrieved image embeddings from dense retrieval \\
    $\mathrm{DINOv2}(\cdot)$ & Function & Model extracting features from images \\
    $\mathrm{Talk2DINO}(\cdot)$ & Function & Model projecting text embeddings into DINOv2 feature space \\
    $\mathrm{Long\text{-}CLIP}(\cdot)$ & Function & Text encoder with extended context window (replaces CLIP text encoder in cross‑modal retrieval) \\
    $C^R$ & Set of communities & Retrieved communities when using graph‑based retrieval \\
    $s_j$ & Scalar ($\mathbb{R}$) & Cosine similarity score between query embedding and community summary $j$ \\
    $a$ & String & Final answer generated by the MLLM \\
    \bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
